## Bugs Encountered (known patterns)

- **Editable-value-vs-reference-value mixup**, same root-cause family as `.Text` vs `.EditValue`: comparing against a fixed "originally requested" field instead of the actual editable field the user just changed. Cost three separate spots in the same file before being caught.
- **Reset Entry not called after save (Known Bug Pattern #6)** — `PrimalCutCosting.cs` didn't refresh its grid after a successful save, leaving stale pre-save state on screen.
- **TVP/DataTable dedup key correctness** — a new variant of care needed with TVPs: a dedup or skip-matching key must be genuinely unique per row, not just "usually good enough" (ProductCode looked sufficient until a same-product-multiple-lines scenario broke it).
- **QUOTED_IDENTIFIER gotcha** — any SP writing to `Inventory` needs `SET QUOTED_IDENTIFIER ON` before its `CREATE PROCEDURE`, since `Inventory` has filtered indexes and that setting is baked in at creation time, not read from the caller's session. Hit this on `spu_UpdatePrimalCutCosting`, consistent with what the existing `sp_FiFoMappingSTS`/`sp_AddBranchOrder` already required.
